Privacy Policy

IF You Uploader · last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains how IF You Uploader (“the app”) handles data. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel IF You... (@IFYouNoir). Its only purpose is to upload and schedule that channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data processed by Google.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in, collect or process data of any other user.

What data the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app can upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time, read the status of those uploads, and delete a scheduled upload that the owner decided not to publish.
  • It reads only the video files and text the owner prepared for those uploads, from the owner's own computer.

The app does not collect personal data from viewers, does not read comments or private messages, and does not use cookies or analytics.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth authorization token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google to call the YouTube API.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is stored on any server operated by the app.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer immediately stops the app from accessing YouTube. Uploaded videos can be removed at any time in YouTube Studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
